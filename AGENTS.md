# Standalone Network Lightcone rules

## Architecture

- The Lightcone is passive causal geometry. It admits exact represented
  network and host-context symbols and emits receipts. Host policy owns any
  allow, reject, quarantine, rate or release action.
- Exact typed graph reachability is authoritative. Learned coordinates and the
  spatial `(x,y,z,w)` observer cannot invent nodes, edges, identity, intent or
  permission.
- Keep protocol mechanisms, implementation observations, unsupported
  inferences and host-policy controls epistemically distinct.

## Clean-world boundary

- Do not put named probes, attacks, malware, exploits, floods, beacons,
  exfiltration behavior or their traffic shapes in authored or compiled
  training worlds.
- Keep such cases in explicitly separate challenge or evaluation artifacts
  that the trainer does not read.
- Unknown observations with no exact represented seed must admit nothing.
  Observations sharing represented protocol symbols may enter the matching
  causal neighborhood; record that overlap honestly.
- Keep replay captures and generated event logs outside authored and compiled
  worlds. Logs describe observation and admission, never a packet verdict.
  Do not log payload bytes by default.

## Evidence and revisions

- Pin sources, rights, hashes, mechanism-level splits, configuration, seed,
  device identity, metrics and artifact hashes.
- Preserve every failed diagnostic, evaluated holdout, superseded pack and
  limitation. Once a holdout is inspected, move it to regression before
  authoring a fresh holdout.
- Never lower a threshold after seeing a held-out result.
- Local CPU or GPU results remain diagnostics. Real Kaggle TPU evidence is
  required for accelerator acceptance.

## Seal prohibition

Do not seal this project until the user explicitly authorizes sealing after
broad coverage, observation adapters, external evaluation, long-duration
reliability and measured defensive effectiveness have been reviewed. Keep
`sealed=false` and every open coverage gate visible until then.
